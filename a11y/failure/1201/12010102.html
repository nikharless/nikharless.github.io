Expected Result

Text transcripts include all dialogue and narration.

Actual Result

Prerecorded audio-only content has a text transcript, but it doesn't include all the dialogue.

Solution

Update the text transcript to include all of the dialogue in the audio.

Passing Example

Transcript

In this example from the This American Life podcast, all spoken dialogue is included in the transcript.

Ira Glass Which got her thinking. And when she got back to work, without much to do, she decided to make a poster of her own.

Jessica Lee Williamson I just wrote “cat found” above two pictures of possums that I had printed off the internet. One of the possums looks like he’s crouching, disturbed by an invasive camera lens. And then the other possum is hissing.

Disabilities Affected

  • Deafness/Hearing Impairment: People who are deaf or hard of hearing need to be able to access the same information provided in the audio.
  • Cognitive Impairment: People who have the ability to hear use transcripts to more easily process the auditory information.
