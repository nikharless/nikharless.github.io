Expected Result

Visual indicators of a switch's boundaries have a contrast ratio of at least 3:1 with the background.

Actual Result

Visual indicators of a switch's boundaries do not have a contrast ratio of at least 3:1 with the background.

Solution

Change the color of the boundary to have a contrast ratio of at least 3:1 with the background.

Failing Example

Passing Example

Border

The borders of both the switch container and the switch thumb have a contrast ratio of at least 3:1 with both the fill and the page background.

Fill

The fills of both the switch container and the switch thumb have a contrast ratio of at least 3:1 with their respective backgrounds.

Disabilities Affected

  • Color Blindness: Users need to be able to distinguish non-text user controls.
  • Visual Impairment: Users need to be able to distinguish non-text user controls.
