Expected Result

Meaningful text shown in the image is conveyed by its text alternative or equivalent text elsewhere in the content.

Actual Result

Assistive technology users do not receive meaningful text shown in the image and may miss information needed to understand it.

Solution

Revise the text alternative to convey any meaningful text shown in the image that is needed to understand its information.

Failing Example

Text alternative does not include meaningful text

Describing this image as "Scrabble tiles on a table" leaves out the meaningful text that they spell out.

How a screen reader reads it:

  • NVDA 2026 / Chrome 158: "graphic, Scrabble tiles on a table"
<img src="carpe-diem.jpg" alt="Scrabble tiles on a table">

Passing Example

Text alternative includes meaningful text

How a screen reader reads it:

  • NVDA 2026 / Chrome 158: "graphic, Scrabble tiles on a table that spell out Carpe Diem"
<img src="carpe-diem.jpg" alt="Scrabble tiles on a table that spell out Carpe Diem">

Disabilities Affected

  • Blindness/Visual Impairment: People who cannot perceive the image need its meaningful text and information conveyed in a form they can access.
  • Cognitive Impairment: People who have difficulty understanding images may benefit from having a text description that accurately describes the image.
